Who We Help

Tshinakaho Haven's intended beneficiaries are orphaned and vulnerable children who require care and support.

These may include children who have:

  • Lost one or both parents.
  • Lost access to a stable family environment.
  • Experienced difficult living circumstances.
  • Required alternative care and support.

Supporting Children's Development

We aim to give children opportunities to learn, develop skills, build confidence and experience a sense of belonging.
