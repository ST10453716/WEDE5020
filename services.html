Our Services

Supporting children through every stage of their development.

Tshinakaho Haven aims to provide more than accommodation. Our services are designed to support the physical, emotional, educational and social needs of every child in our care.

Through a safe and caring environment, we aim to give children the support and opportunities they need to grow with confidence.

What We Provide

Our Approach

Supporting the whole child

Every child's needs are different. Tshinakaho Haven aims to create an environment that considers children's physical, emotional, educational and social development together.

Physical Wellbeing

A safe home, nutritious food, clothing and personal care.

Education

Learning support, school resources and opportunities to develop academically.

Emotional Wellbeing

A caring environment where children feel valued, respected and supported.

Personal Development

Recreation, social development and life skills that help children prepare for their future.
