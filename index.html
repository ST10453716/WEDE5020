A Safe Place To Belong

Tshinakaho Haven is a proposed non-profit children's home in South Africa dedicated to providing a safe, caring and supportive environment for orphaned and vulnerable children.

Our aim is to create a family-like home where children feel loved, respected, valued and supported as they grow.

About Tshinakaho Haven Get Involved

Our Purpose

Creating a safe and supportive home.

Tshinakaho Haven aims to provide more than accommodation. We want to create an environment where vulnerable children receive the care, support and opportunities they need to develop physically, emotionally, socially and academically.

About Tshinakaho Haven

What We Provide

Supporting every part of a child's development.

Our proposed children's home services focus on creating a stable environment where children can feel safe, continue their education and develop the skills they need for the future.

Safe Accommodation

A stable, comfortable and caring place where children can feel protected and at home.

Food & Nutrition

Nutritious meals that support children's health, wellbeing and development.

Educational Support

Homework assistance, school supplies, learning resources and a suitable environment for studying.

Personal Care

Clothing and everyday personal care essentials that promote dignity and wellbeing.

Emotional Support

A caring environment where children feel heard, respected, valued and supported.

Life Skills

Opportunities to build confidence, relationships and practical skills for greater independence.
