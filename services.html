Our Services

Tshinakaho Haven aims to support children's physical, emotional, educational and social development.

Accommodation

A safe and comfortable place for children to live.

Food and Nutrition

Nutritious meals to support health and development.

Educational Support

School supplies, learning assistance and educational resources.

Clothing and Personal Care

Clothing, toiletries and personal-care necessities.

Emotional Support

A caring environment where children can feel heard and supported.

Recreation and Development

Sports, arts, music, games and developmental activities.

Life Skills

Support to prepare older children for adulthood and independence.
