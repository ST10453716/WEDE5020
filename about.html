About Tshinakaho Haven

Creating a safe place for children to grow, learn and belong.

Tshinakaho Haven is a proposed non-profit children's home in South Africa that aims to provide a safe, caring and supportive environment for orphaned and vulnerable children.

Our goal is to create more than a place to stay. We want children to experience a family-like environment where they feel loved, respected, valued and supported.

Our Purpose

Helping children build a brighter future

Tshinakaho Haven aims to support the physical, emotional, educational and social development of vulnerable children through a safe and supportive home environment.

We believe that every child deserves a safe environment where they can develop with confidence and dignity.

Through accommodation, nutrition, education, emotional support, recreation and life skills, Tshinakaho Haven aims to give children the support and opportunities they need to grow.

Where We Are Going

Our Vision & Mission

01

Our Vision

Our vision is for every child to have a safe place to call home, to feel loved and valued, and to have the opportunity to reach their full potential.

We believe that a child's circumstances should not determine their future.

02

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, loving and supportive home that responds to children's physical, emotional and educational needs.

We aim to help children develop into confident, responsible and independent individuals.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These values guide the type of environment Tshinakaho Haven aims to create for every child.

Love & Compassion

Treating every child with kindness, understanding and genuine care.

Safety

Creating a secure and stable environment where children can feel protected.

Respect

Recognising the dignity, individuality and worth of every child.

Family & Belonging

Building an environment where children feel accepted, included and at home.

Empowerment

Helping children develop confidence, independence and skills for their future.

Integrity

Acting responsibly, honestly and with the best interests of children in mind.
