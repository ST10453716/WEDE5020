Welcome to Tshinakaho Haven

Tshinakaho Haven is a non-profit children's home based in Midrand, Gauteng. We aim to provide a safe, caring and supportive home for orphaned and vulnerable children.

We aim to create a family-like environment where every child feels loved, safe, respected, valued, accepted and supported.

What We Provide

  • Safe accommodation
  • Food and nutrition
  • Educational support
  • Clothing and personal care
  • Emotional support
  • Recreation and development
  • Life skills
