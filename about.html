About Us

Tshinakaho Haven is a non-profit children's home based in Midrand, Gauteng, South Africa. The organisation aims to provide a safe, caring and supportive environment for orphaned and vulnerable children.

Our Mission

To provide orphaned and vulnerable children with a safe, loving and supportive home while meeting their physical, emotional and educational needs.

Our Vision

To create a world where every child has a safe place to call home, feels loved and valued, and has the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Our Core Values

  • Love and compassion
  • Safety
  • Respect
  • Family and belonging
  • Empowerment
  • Integrity
